# Book recommendations skill

I created the skill to get book recommendations based on content warnings. But I discovered as I wrote the system instructions that I needed the full book text for the skill to work. Review sites didn’t have enough information. Also, the more I tested the skill, the more defects I found in the project. I realized the project’s research question itself had a flaw, and stopped in the middle.

I included the case study and the rest of the project here to show my thought process.
