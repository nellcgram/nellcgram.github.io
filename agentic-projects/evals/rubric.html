Rubric

Grading outcomes

Each criterion is graded pass, fail, or unverifiable.

  • Pass — positive evidence the criterion is met.
  • Fail — positive evidence the criterion is violated.
  • Unverifiable — the evidence needed to decide is not available from the sources used. Not a pass. Every unverifiable grade must name the entry, the missing evidence, and what would settle it.

Unverifiable is counted separately in results and never folded into the pass count.

If any entry in a criterion is unverifiable, the whole criterion is graded unverifiable.

Minimum 15 books returned

  • Pass: 15+ books, or explanation why fewer
  • Fail: Fewer than 15 with no explanation

Books and authors must be real and correctly attributed

  • Pass: All books exist, have the correct title and author, and all authors are real
  • Fail: Any book does not exist, has the wrong author, or any author does not exist

Main character has no pregnancy or child before the epilogue

  • Pass: Main characters are not parents or guardians of a child during the book and no main character is pregnant before the epilogue; an epilogue baby or pregnancy is optional
  • Fail: A main character has a child, is a guardian of a child, or is pregnant at any point before the epilogue

No books or authors from already-read.md

  • Pass: None of the books or authors from already-read.md appear in the results
  • Fail: Any book or author from already-read.md appears in the results

No books or authors from the "skip all books by these authors" list

  • Source of truth: this list is not defined in SKILL.md — it's the "## skip all books by these authors" section of already-read.md (currently /Users/nellgram/.claude/private-notes/book-recommendations/already-read.md). Grade against that section, not against SKILL.md.
  • Pass: None of the books or authors from the skip list appear in the results
  • Fail: Any book or author from the skip list appears in the results

No author appears twice within one set of results

  • Pass: No author appears more than once in the same set of results
  • Fail: Any author appears more than once in the same set of results

No novellas

  • Pass: Every recommended title is a full-length novel
  • Fail: Any recommended title is a novella

No prohibited main-character types

  • Pass: No main character is a criminal, mafia member, vampire, demon, alien, or serial killer
  • Fail: Any main character is one of the above

Content warnings follow the required format and coverage

  • Pass: Every entry with rape, torture, murder, cheating, BDSM, cancer, a main character's dog dying, or a main character with PTSD/other mental illness includes a content-warning line naming it; entries with none of those omit the line entirely; any rape/sexual-assault warning uses the format TYPE (ROLE, TIMING) with TYPE = "rape" or "sexual assault (not rape)", ROLE = "main character" or "secondary character", TIMING = "on page" or "past"; rape, torture, and murder are only present when not committed by a main character
  • Fail: A qualifying content warning is missing, a content-warning line appears with nothing to warn about, a sexual-violence warning doesn't match the required format, or a main character commits rape, torture, or murder
  • Unverifiable: Review-site summaries mention potentially qualifying content but don't confirm whether it occurs, who it happens to, or whether it's on page. Settled by: the book text.

Closed-door/open-door heat level noted correctly

  • Pass: A closed-door book includes a "Heat level: Closed door." line; an open-door book includes no heat-level line at all
  • Fail: An open-door book includes a heat-level line, or a closed-door book has no heat-level line
  • Unverifiable: No source consulted states whether the book is open or closed door. Settled by: the book text, or a review that explicitly names heat level.

Every recommendation includes a link

  • Pass: Every book entry includes a link to the title
  • Fail: Any entry has no link

Exclusion process kept invisible

  • Pass: The response never mentions already-read.md, the skip-authors list, or why a specific book/author was excluded
  • Fail: The response references the exclusion list, already-read status, or explains that something was skipped
