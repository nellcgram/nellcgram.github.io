# About

Hi, I'm Nell. I care about how information is organized and how to make it easier for people and AI systems to find and use it. My love of words is also a cornerstone of my life. These combined interests led to a BA in English, an MS in Library and Information Science, 8+ years of work in technical and digital content, and several years before that working in libraries.

This site began as a portfolio of writing samples and I'm expanding it to include in-progress agentic AI projects. I'm also practicing using a docs-as-code workflow.
