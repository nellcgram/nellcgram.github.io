# About me

Hi, I'm Nell. At work, I'm the person who finds the exact wording for a system instruction, adds the missing metadata, and fixes the heading hierarchy. Outside work, I'd reorganize your pantry by category for fun.

I care about how information gets organized, why it gets lost, and how to make it easier for people and AI systems to find and use.

I specialize in information architecture: classification systems, metadata, and content models. I have a Master's in Library and Information Science and 8+ years of experience in technical and digital content, including documentation for Google Play's 2 billion users and accessible course content for 8,750+ students at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

This site will be updated with agentic AI projects as I complete them.
