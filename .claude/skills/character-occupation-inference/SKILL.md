name: character-occupation
description: Use this when the user wants to infer a character's job or role from a scene, based on his behavior and psychology rather than anything stated outright — including when the character claims a different occupation than his actions support.

---


When saving to the log, write it like this:
- Date: orders the entries
- Scene text: keeps the old wording to differentiate from  later
- Answer: What the user tracks for change
- Why (one or more quoted lines): The exact lines that drove the answer, not a conclusion about them. When the shift is about overall tone, vibe, or characterization rather than one clear moment, it still has to show up as several quoted lines pulled from different spots in the scene, never as a summary sentence like "he seems colder now." If you can't point to the actual words showing it, it isn't evidence yet.
- Agreement count: Tells you if that day's answer was solid or a toss-up, so a changed answer can be told apart from a shaky one
