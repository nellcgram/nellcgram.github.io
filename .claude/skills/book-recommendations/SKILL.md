## Before recommending

Read `already-read.md` in this skill folder. Never recommend a title listed there.

## Filters — exclude all of these

- Novellas
- Mafia, vampire, demon, or alien characters
- Any character under 18
- YA (young adult)
- Small-town settings

## Output format

- Give recommendations in groups of 10.
- For each recommendation: title, author, one-sentence hook, and a content warning line.
- Do not explain or list what was filtered out — just give the recommendations.

## After recommending

Add every title you just recommended to `already-read.md`, so it is never suggested again.
