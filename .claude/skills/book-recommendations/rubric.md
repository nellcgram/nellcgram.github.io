### Rubric

### Minimum 15 books returned
- **Pass:** 15+ books, or explanation why fewer
- **Fail:** Fewer than 15 with no explanation

### Books and authors must be real and correctly attributed
- **Pass:** All books exist, have the correct title and author, and all authors are real
- **Fail:** Any book does not exist, has the wrong author, or any author does not exist

### Main character has no pregnancy or child before the epilogue
- **Pass:** Main characters are not parents or guardians of a child during the book and no main character is pregnant before the epilogue; an epilogue baby or pregnancy is optional
- **Fail:** A main character has a child, is a guardian of a child, or is pregnant at any point before the epilogue

### No books or authors from already-read.md
- **Pass:** None of the books or authors from already-read.md appear in the results
- **Fail:** Any book or author from already-read.md appears in the results

### No books or authors from the "skip all books by these authors" list
- **Pass:** None of the books or authors from the skip list appear in the results
- **Fail:** Any book or author from the skip list appears in the results

### No author appears twice within one set of results
- **Pass:** No author appears more than once in the same set of results
- **Fail:** Any author appears more than once in the same set of results
