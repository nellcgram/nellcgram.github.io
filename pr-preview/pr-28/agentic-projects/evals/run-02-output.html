    Run-02 Output [2026-09-10]

    Genre note: the skill's format section says to ask which genre if the user doesn't specify one. This run had no live user turn to ask, so it was not asked — the list below mixes contemporary and fantasy romance (both covered by the skill's README) instead of one genre picked for the user. That's a deviation from a real run and is called out in run-02.md rather than silently matched to Run 1's assumption.

    The Ex Hex, Erin Sterling. Genre: Fantasy A witch curses her ex-boyfriend the night he leaves her, and nine years later he's back in town just as everything in his life starts going wrong.

    Icebreaker, Hannah Grace. Genre: Contemporary A competitive figure skater is forced to share ice time with the college hockey team's captain, and their rivalry turns into something else.

    The Fine Print, Lauren Asher. Genre: Contemporary An amusement-park heir who never shows his face publicly hires a passionate employee to help save the park, and their arrangement gets personal. Content warnings: Main character with PTSD or other mental illness

    The Cul-de-Sac War, Melissa Ferguson. Genre: Contemporary An actress who inherits her grandmother's house finds her new neighbor insufferable, and their escalating pranks blur the line between hate and attraction. Heat level: Closed door.

    A Lot Like Adiós, Alexis Daria. Genre: Contemporary A freelance graphic designer hires her childhood best friend's gym for a marketing campaign, and old feelings resurface as they work together.

    Two Wrongs Make a Right, Chloe Liese. Genre: Contemporary Set up on a disastrous blind date by their meddling friends, a pediatrician and a stationery designer agree to fake-date badly enough to end the matchmaking for good. Content warnings: Main character with PTSD or other mental illness

    Not Like the Movies, Kerry Winfrey. Genre: Contemporary

    A coffee-shop waitress is horrified when her best friend turns their nonexistent romance with her boss into a hit movie script, forcing her to see him differently.

    Heat level: Closed door.

    The Dating Plan, Sara Desai. Genre: Contemporary A software engineer and the family friend who once stood her up agree to a fake engagement that benefits them both, and pretend dates start feeling real.

    The Spellshop, Sarah Beth Durst. Genre: Fantasy A reclusive librarian flees a burning library with a stash of illegal spellbooks and opens a secret shop on her childhood island home, where a persistent neighbor keeps showing up. Heat level: Closed door.

    By the Book, Jasmine Guillory. Genre: Contemporary An overworked editorial assistant is assigned to whip a difficult, demanding author's overdue manuscript into shape, and finds someone unexpectedly sweet underneath.

    Funny You Should Ask, Elissa Sussman. Genre: Contemporary A journalist's puff-piece profile of her celebrity crush turns into a whirlwind weekend, and a follow-up interview ten years later reopens everything.

    A Novel Love Story, Ashley Poston. Genre: Fantasy A literature professor gets stranded in the small town from her favorite unfinished romance series, frozen in time until she helps resolve its last story.

    Just Like Magic, Sarah Hogle. Genre: Fantasy A washed-up influencer accidentally conjures the literal spirit of the holidays into human form and starts using his wish-granting to fix her life. Heat level: Closed door.

    The Hookup Plan, Farrah Rochon. Genre: Contemporary A pediatric surgeon looking for a no-strings arrangement ends up hooking up with her high school rival, who's now assessing whether her hospital should be sold.

    The Duke Who Didn't, Courtney Milan. Genre: Historical A duke who never told his childhood best friend he owns her entire village returns to finally court her properly, sauce recipes and all.
