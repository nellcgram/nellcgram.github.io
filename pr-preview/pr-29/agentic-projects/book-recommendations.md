## Book Recommendations Skill

I created the skill to get book recommendations based on content warnings. But I discovered as I wrote the system instructions that I needed the full book text for the skill to work; review sites didn’t have enough information. Also, the more I tested the skill, the more defects I found in the project. I realized the project’s research question itself was flawed, and stopped in the middle.

The case study and the rest of the project are included here to show my thought process.
