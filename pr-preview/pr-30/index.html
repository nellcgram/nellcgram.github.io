About me

Hi, I'm Nell. I care about how information gets organized and how to make it easier for people and AI systems to find and use it.

Words are also a cornerstone of my life. My interests led to 8+ years of work in technical and digital content (most recently), a BA in English, and an MS in Library and Information Science. Between the BA and the MS I spent several years working in libraries.

This site holds both my technical writing portfolio and in-progress agentic AI projects. I'm also practicing using a docs-as-code workflow.

Personal

I'm a big reader, fiction writer, and sometimes-yogi. I'm also teaching myself about API documentation for fun.
